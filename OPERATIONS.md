# Human-governed case workflow

The governed case API accepts verified, provenance-linked records under a documented consent/lawful-basis reference and rejects protected attributes. Its policy step checks completeness and staleness only: it never produces liberty, pricing, eligibility, or adverse decisions. Final outcomes require a separate authorized human, signed policy attestation, case rationale, and an adverse-action explanation where applicable. Disputes and immutable audit events are durable.

Bootstrap/migrations/guarded seed are explicit; start does not mutate data. Model/agentic and generated gap decision routes are unmounted. Court/identity/bureau/payment/communications/case integrations, jurisdictional review, calibration/fairness/drift studies, licensed-agent approval, and legal validation remain mandatory external work. This software is not legal advice or authorization for a bail decision.
